# Support

This project is maintained in spare time and offered as is, with no service level. Replies are best effort, with no
promise of when.

## Where to ask

- A bug, a feature idea, or advice from the pool that turned out wrong: open a GitHub issue and pick the matching
  template.
- A question about how something works: read `README.md` and `docs/` first (`docs/troubleshooting.md` lists real
  failure messages), then open an issue if the answer is not there.
- A security problem: do not open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- A conduct concern: follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## What to include

- The version (`lodgings --version`, or the commit you cloned), your operating system, and your Python version.
- The exact command you ran and what it printed, as plain text output rather than a screenshot.
- Which kind of hosts are involved (a role such as "Windows laptop" is enough) and what you expected instead. This
  project shows unknown and stale facts as such, so say which one you saw.
- No secrets: no tokens, keys or passwords. Redact host names, accounts and paths first; a recorded fixture with invented
  names is better than a paste from your own machines.
